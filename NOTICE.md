# Third-party material and assistance

The original implementation, fixtures, experiment scripts and documentation in
this project are released under the root MIT license. The root license does not
replace third-party licenses.

`vendor/sentinel-tpd/` is an unmodified subset of SENTINEL-TPD at
`5ce5d56e57a0acd092435fca5d89b78d0b39ee7b`; its MIT license is retained and
`vendor/manifest.json` records Git blob and SHA-256 identities for every file.
The upstream package and this study have the same human author; comparator
results must therefore be interpreted with that author-affiliation limitation.
The study's wrappers are newly authored adapters, not upstream product behavior.

`manuscript/IEEEtran.cls` comes from the official EuroS&P 2027 template.
Its original copyright and LPPL notices remain in the file. The official template
archive URL and hashes are in `submission/template-provenance.json`.

No fuzzd or MCPTox dataset is included or needed. All newly generated descriptors
and event traces are inert synthetic fixtures authored for this study. They are
not a sample of real-world attacks.

OpenAI Codex assisted with literature retrieval, code and fixture generation,
analysis, testing, and manuscript drafting. Exact API model snapshots were not
recorded and no independent human peer review is claimed. Researcher review and
the applicable venue's disclosure requirements remain the human author's
responsibility. Authorship is not assigned to an AI system.
